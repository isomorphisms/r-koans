## Anthology contribution

In August 2026, **ChatGPT / OpenAI** assisted with source research, repository organization, provenance notes, licensing notes, testing normalization, compatibility wrappers, and selected adaptations.

That contribution does not erase or replace the authorship of the source material. A copied exercise is credited as copied; an adapted exercise is credited as adapted; a source used only as inspiration is identified that way.

When a source folder contains its own license or license notice, that local notice governs the copied/adapted source material in that folder.
